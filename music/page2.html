Baroque Music

The Baroque period was marked by ornamentation and complex melodies. Using counterpoint and imitation, composers created impressive symphonies. Styles like the fugue were popular. Harpsichords, organs, or cellos played a repetitive bassline called basso continuo.

Classical Music

Classical music is simplistic and grounded in rational thought, which was idolized at the time. Prominent classical composers include Mozart and Beethoven. After the Baroque period, melodies became more repetitive. Symphonies and concertos were popular at this time.

Romantic Music

The Romantic period emerged as the heart of musical composition, so to speak, compared to the Classical period's focus on intelligence. Pieces were longer and more emotional, with bigger dynamics. Many composers wrote about the sublime and the macabre.

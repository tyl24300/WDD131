Overview

Purpose

In my Music and the Humanities class, we have been learning about music in different eras of the world. I think it would be really cool to have an interactive website with information on these eras, playable audios, and a way to search a database of music/music periods.

Audience

My audience would be people in the 18-40 range looking to learn more about music, and hear music that comes from a variety of eras and places.

Dynamic elements

I will use an array to store different styles of music, with a searchbar and submit button for input. I will also make the page responsive to phone and tablet screens. I will use a database to fill in which music is displayed as well.

Styles like the fugue were popular at this time.
